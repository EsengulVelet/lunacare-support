Privacy policy

Your records are yours. Here is how LunaCare handles them and the choices available to you.

Publisher: Esengül Velet · LunaCare 1.0.0

Records and their purpose

The optional name or nickname, logged period days, water intake, mood, notes, reminder titles/descriptions/dates/recurrence, daily completion marks and app preferences are processed on your device. They support personal greetings, daily views, the planner, cycle estimates and local notifications. You can use record features without providing a name or granting notification permission.

Storage and transmission

On iOS, records are stored in a file encrypted with Apple CryptoKit. The key is kept in the device Keychain and is device-specific. LunaCare does not send these records to a server operated by LunaCare. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or user tracking system.

Local notifications are scheduled on the device by iOS. Reminder titles can appear in notifications; private descriptions and notes do not. “Hide notification content” in Settings also hides titles. Cycle notifications use generic content by default. System permissions and lock-screen previews also depend on your iPhone settings.

Export

My records and privacy lets you share a readable JSON copy. The file is not encrypted and may contain personal or health-related records. The app, cloud service or person you select may access its contents; you initiate the transfer. The recipient service has its own privacy terms. Deleting records in LunaCare does not delete files previously saved or shared.

Backup and recovery limits

This version offers no automatic cloud backup, file import or restore. Because the encryption key is device-specific, changing devices, deleting the app or losing the key may cause loss of access to records. The readable export is not offered as a backup that can be restored into the app.

Deletion and preferences

You can manage records in the app and delete all local records after explicit confirmation. Bulk deletion cannot be undone and associated local notifications are removed. Previously exported copies must be deleted separately where you stored or shared them. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings; feature and language preferences can be changed in LunaCare.

Apple services and support

iOS, App Store and TestFlight are subject to Apple’s privacy practices. Feedback you submit through TestFlight and the operating system’s own diagnostics sharing do not mean LunaCare sends your records to its own server.

You do not need to include health records or an exported file in a support request. Contact LunaCare Support at lunacare.supportt@gmail.com. Email addresses and message contents are processed by the email service.

Visiting this website

This support website cannot access your app records. It has no forms, advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts. The hosting provider may process network requests to deliver and secure the website under its own service terms.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when features or data flows change. The current date appears above. Questions: lunacare.supportt@gmail.com.

Please include your app version, iPhone model and the steps that led to the issue. You do not need to send health records.

Missing a notification?

Check Settings → Notifications → LunaCare on your iPhone. Enable “Remind me” for the reminder and choose a future notification time. Focus, silent mode and notification preview settings may affect delivery, appearance and sound. “Hide notification content” in LunaCare hides reminder titles. Opening LunaCare refreshes future reminder schedules.

Where are records stored?

Personal records are encrypted on your iPhone. This version does not sync them to a LunaCare server and does not offer automatic cloud backup or importing a file. Deleting the app or changing devices may cause loss of access to your records. Contact support before deleting the app if you have a problem.

Exporting or deleting records

In Settings → My records and privacy, you can export or delete local records. Export creates a readable, unencrypted JSON file. Store it securely. It cannot be imported back into this version of the app. Deleting local records does not delete previously exported or shared files.

Changing language

LunaCare initially follows your iPhone language: Turkish, or English for other languages. Tap the round TR/EN button at the top right of Settings to change language. Your choice is saved. Names, notes and reminder titles you write are not translated.

Cycle and moon information

Cycle dates and phases are estimates based on your calendar records. They do not confirm ovulation and must not be used for medical assessment or contraception. Moon phases are approximate calculations. Lunar interpretations and hair-care suggestions are for inspiration and entertainment.

Contact privacy

When you email us, your address and message are processed by the email service. Please leave out private information that is not needed to explain your issue.
